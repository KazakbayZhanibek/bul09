The original website supplied threadlab-fixed.glb, an optimized derivative of
this garment with simplified panels, meshopt compression and a plain material.

The BUL BUL CORP field-tee.glb adaptation reshapes and subdivides those panels
for a wider, dropped-shoulder fit. It adds sculpted folds, an undulating hem,
modeled rib-knit neck binding, and two rows of raised stitching at the cuffs
and hem. Shoulder stitching, fabric UVs and the repeating cotton jersey normal
and roughness maps were prepared for this collection. The website changes the
cloth color and applies the collection's existing front and back artwork.

Source geometry and modifications are identified here for redistribution.
The model illustrates a collection concept. The source author does not
endorse the adaptation.
